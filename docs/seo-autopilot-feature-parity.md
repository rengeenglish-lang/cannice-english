## Netfener-specific additions

Real exam/product grounding; cannibalization checks; content version history; active-admin authorization; auditable decisions; AI cost reservations and monthly caps; approval gates; emergency pause; Search Console snapshots; organic registration/practice/purchase attribution; business-value scoring.

## Rollout

1. Deploy the verified foundation only after CI and browser checks pass. No paid generation or automatic publication.
2. Build and verify the content engine: keywords, opportunities, editable brand profile/briefs, manual editing and provider-backed generation. Connect a user-selected AI provider only with server-side credentials and an explicit budget. Unconfigured integrations must remain visibly unavailable.
3. Continue the original brief's intelligence, publishing, performance, attribution, automation and competitor phases one at a time. Maintain this table with evidence, not marketing completion labels.
4. Treat external CMS connectors as a separate expansion of the native publisher; configuration and credentials for each target are required. Do not transmit content to third parties or publish to unspecified properties.

A deploy authorization is not authorization to invent search-volume data, turn on unlimited AI spending, fabricate analytics, or publish unchecked articles. Full autopilot remains opt-in after its safeguards have passed tests.
